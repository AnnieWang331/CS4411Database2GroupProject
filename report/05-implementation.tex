\paragraph{WebSocket connects skip the check on purpose.} A whole class connects at once, so a
room check on every connect would add to the conflicts on the room item
(Section~\ref{sec:dm-rules}). Instead, the \texttt{connect} function in
\texttt{connections.py} writes the \texttt{WS\#} and \texttt{CONN\#} items without a room
condition and then reads the room again with a strongly consistent read~\cite{read-consistency}.
If the room has closed by then, it deletes both items and refuses the connection. Otherwise any
later close comes after our write, so the stream consumer will find the connection and tell it.
