\section{Full Access-Pattern Table}\label{app:access-patterns}

Table~\ref{tab:access-patterns-full} lists all 21 access patterns from
\texttt{docs/data-model.md}, Section~1. The pattern numbers also appear in the docstrings of
\texttt{src/brainstorm/rooms.py}, \texttt{ideas.py} and \texttt{connections.py}, so each row can be
traced to the function that implements it. Every key is in the room's partition
\texttt{ROOM\#\textless room\_id\textgreater} unless the row says otherwise.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \footnotesize
  \caption{All access patterns of the brainstorm board and the operation that serves each.
    ``Desc.'' means newest first (\texttt{ScanIndexForward=False}). ``Transaction'' means
    \texttt{TransactWriteItems}; its conditions are in Table~\ref{tab:rules}.}
  \label{tab:access-patterns-full}
  \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}r>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.9cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.9cm}@{}}
    \toprule
    P & What the app does & Operation and key & Function \\
    \midrule
    1 & Create a room with a unique join code & Transaction: put room and
      \texttt{CODE\#\textless code\textgreater} item & \texttt{create\_room} \\
    2 & Find a room from its join code & \texttt{GetItem} on
      \texttt{CODE\#\textless code\textgreater}, \texttt{META}, strongly
      consistent~\cite{read-consistency}; then P3; reject if expired & \texttt{resolve\_code} \\
    3 & Get a room's details & \texttt{GetItem} on \texttt{META}; reject if expired &
      \texttt{get\_room} \\
    4 & Join a room & Transaction: room check, add or update \texttt{USER\#\textless
      user\_id\textgreater} & \texttt{join\_room} \\
    5 & List a room's participants & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{begins\_with(SK,
      \textquotesingle USER\#\textquotesingle{})} & \texttt{list\_participants} \\
    6 & Post an idea & Transaction: room check, put \texttt{IDEA\#} item & \texttt{post\_idea} \\
    7 & Load the board & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{SK BETWEEN \textquotesingle IDEA\#\textquotesingle{}
      AND \textquotesingle META\textquotesingle{}}, desc. & \texttt{load\_board} \\
    8 & Load the board with comments & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{SK \textless=
      \textquotesingle META\textquotesingle{}}, desc. & \texttt{load\_board} \\
    9 & Page through ideas & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{begins\_with(SK,
      \textquotesingle IDEA\#\textquotesingle{})}, desc., \texttt{Limit} (default 20) &
      \texttt{list\_ideas} \\
    10 & Show the top-voted ideas & No call: sort the ideas from P7 by \texttt{vote\_count} in the
      app & \texttt{top\_ideas} \\
    11 & Vote (once per user per idea) & Transaction: room check, put vote marker, add 1 to
      \texttt{vote\_count} & \texttt{vote} \\
    12 & Take a vote back & Transaction: room check, delete vote marker, subtract 1 &
      \texttt{unvote} \\
    13 & See which ideas I voted for & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{begins\_with(SK,
      \textquotesingle VOTE\#\textless user\_id\textgreater\#\textquotesingle{})} &
      \texttt{my\_votes} \\
    14 & Comment on an idea & Transaction: room check, put comment, add 1 to
      \texttt{comment\_count} & \texttt{add\_comment} \\
    15 & Read the comments on one idea & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{begins\_with(SK,
      \textquotesingle COMMENT\#\textless idea\_id\textgreater\#\textquotesingle{})}, oldest
      first & \texttt{list\_comments} \\
    16 & Close a room & Transaction: mark room closed, delete its code item &
      \texttt{close\_room} \\
    17 & An instructor's rooms & \texttt{Query} on GSI1, \texttt{GSI1PK =
      USER\#\textless owner\_id\textgreater}, desc.; eventually consistent~\cite{gsi}; expired

    18 & Delete old rooms & No call: TTL deletes items after \texttt{expires\_at}~\cite{ttl} & --- \\
    19 & Connect a WebSocket to a room & Strongly consistent P3, transaction puts
      \texttt{WS\#\textless connection\_id\textgreater} and
      \texttt{CONN\#\textless connection\_id\textgreater}, strongly consistent P3 again &
      \texttt{connect} \\
    20 & Disconnect a WebSocket & \texttt{GetItem} on
      \texttt{CONN\#\textless connection\_id\textgreater}, strongly consistent; transaction
      deletes both items & \texttt{disconnect} \\
    21 & List a room's connections (live updates) & \texttt{Query}, \texttt{begins\_with(SK,
      \textquotesingle WS\#\textquotesingle{})}, strongly consistent; expired ones skipped except
      for \texttt{room\_expired} & \texttt{list\_connections} \\

  \end{tabularx}
